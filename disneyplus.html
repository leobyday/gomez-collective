The project

Disney+ had a hard deadline to reach 30+ countries in two years, with an American product built for a single language, culture, and content expectation.

The challenge wasn't localization, it was building an internationalization framework flexible enough to serve Tokyo, São Paulo, Sydney, and Paris without fragmenting into dozens of disconnected experiences.

Every US product decision had downstream consequences for every market that followed, at the speed of a startup, at the quality bar of one of the world's most recognized brands.
