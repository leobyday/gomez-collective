Impact

Ran A/B experiments across the Homescreen to optimize content discovery, personalization, and streaming engagement, averaging a 15 to 18% increase in streaming hours for OTT content.

Insights from Users

Roku's Home Screen is very important for our users: Home Screen Sentiment is one of the best predictors of CSAT, even better than Platform Streaming Hours or Account Tenure.

Based on the user's survey on perception: 77% Simplicity, 51% Helpfulness, 15% Delight.

A/B tests

In order to increase delight and helpfulness perception in the product, we ran a series of A/B tests that would break down specifics of our home screen, without changing its simplicity.

Homescreen

Once we had insights from the tests, we iterated on a different paradigm that would address the new insights and business needs.

The new Homescreen is a balance of research, design evolution, product and business goals.

What's Internationalization?

Internationalization is nuanced insights and products adapted to users all over the world. Different from localization, which focuses on translations, orthography and cultural insights or globalization which are the tools and systems that help products scale.

Strategy

Launching the hardware in new markets, in Australia, Europe and LATAM. Update the experience to meet the compliance needs for each country and region, which included, but were not limited to: parental controls, privacy and settings, Live TV set up and accessibility requirements, such as closed captions/subtitles and audio tracks.
