HOMESCREEN

The Redesign

The Homescreen serves multiple stakeholders with competing priorities: Ads needs real estate for promoted content, Discovery wants algorithmic rows to surface new titles, OTA partners require channel scan and live TV placement, and OTT services need prominent app tiles.

Every pixel is contested. The redesign had to balance all four teams without letting any one priority dominate, while keeping the simplicity score that users consistently ranked as Roku's most important quality.

INTERNATIONALIZATION

What's Internationalization?

Internationalization is the practice of adapting products for users across the world. Going beyond localization, which handles translation, orthography, and cultural nuance, and beyond globalization, which focuses on the systems and tools that enable scale. True internationalization sits at the intersection of all three: understanding how people in different markets think, behave, and expect products to work.

STRATEGY

Strategy

Led the design for Roku's hardware expansion into Australia, Europe, and LATAM, adapting the core experience to meet the compliance and regulatory requirements of each market. This included parental controls, privacy and settings, Live TV configuration, and accessibility standards such as closed captions, subtitle formatting, and audio track management. Each region brought distinct legal and cultural constraints that required more than translation; they required rethinking how core features were surfaced and controlled.
