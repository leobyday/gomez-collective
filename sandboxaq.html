Human-in-the-loop is an expectation, not a fallback

When it comes to AI, human-in-the-loop is how models get better, not just how they stay safe. Every correction a domain expert makes is a training signal. Every decision a researcher overrides becomes a constraint that sharpens the next output. The products that win in high-stakes domains are the ones that put people in the right place within the process, turning their judgment into higher-quality data, stronger models, and outputs worth trusting.

I design and build 0-to-1 products at that intersection: complex systems where AI does the heavy computation and people bring the judgment that data alone can't provide. The two case studies below show that work.
